## 3. SWOT Analysis (Tamil Nadu context)

### Strengths
- **All-in-One Localized Integration**: Combining scale readings + SCADA batching + GPS + local GST e-invoicing in one platform.
- **M-Sand Calibration Focus**: Built-in moisture correction formulas designed for Tamil Nadu's high-silt M-sand profiles.
- **Multi-lingual Mobile Apps**: Driver apps fully translated to Tamil (தமிழ்) with voice directions.

### Weaknesses
- **New Entrant Status**: Overcoming builder trust compared to legacy paper bills.
- **Support Demands**: High support load due to 24/7 night pour shifts common in Chennai/Coimbatore.

### Opportunities
- **Chennai Infrastructure Build**: High demand for digital QA cube reports by Chennai Metro contractors.
- **Aggregate Integration**: Opportunity to connect sand/aggregate quarry owners with local concrete plants.

### Threats
- **Hardware Tampering**: Plant workers deliberately disabling GPS/drum sensors to bypass fuel audits.
- **Local Union Friction**: Truck drivers unionizing against tracking software.
